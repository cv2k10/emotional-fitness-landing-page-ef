01 / Notice where you are

A starting point, not a score.

Take a quiet moment with each statement. Mark the response that feels most true for you lately. There are no right answers, and this is not a diagnostic tool. Use what you notice as information, not a judgement.

I can notice when pressure is beginning to affect how I feel.

I can make a little space between a strong feeling and my response.

I can switch my attention away from work when I choose to.

I can name what I need in a demanding moment.

My decisions reflect what matters to me, as well as what is urgent.

02 / Recognise your patterns

Five prompts for a clearer picture.

You do not need to answer all five at once. Choose the prompt that feels most useful today and write without trying to find a perfect answer.

1. When does my energy change?What tends to happen just before I feel more stretched, distracted, or depleted?
2. What is my first response under pressure?Do I speed up, withdraw, take over, seek reassurance, or do something else?
3. Which conversations stay with me?What might they reveal about a value, boundary, or need that matters?
4. Where do I make room for recovery?What helps me transition out of work, even briefly?
5. What would a thoughtful response look like?In one current situation, what might I choose if I had a little more space?

03 / A simple daily practice

Pause. Notice. Choose.

Try this brief check-in at a natural transition in your day: before a meeting, after a difficult conversation, or when you close your laptop. Take as much or as little time as feels useful.

01
PauseLet your attention settle for a moment. Feel your feet on the floor or notice one steady breath.
02
NameWhat emotion, sensation, or thought is most present right now? Use ordinary words; there is no need to fix it.
03
AskWhat might this moment be asking of me? Is there a need, value, or boundary worth noticing?
04
ChooseWhat is one response I can take intentionally, rather than automatically?
05
ReflectAfterwards, note what you learned. A useful practice is one you can return to, not one you have to get perfect.
